Rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties

On a complex abelian variety with complex multiplication, is every Hodge class a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic cycles?

Model / AI: OpenAI (unreleased)

•self-reported
The claimed result covers CM abelian varieties, which is a structured subclass rather than the full general Hodge conjecture. Shortly after release, OpenAI retracted a related companion manuscript regarding K3 surfaces due to a sign error, underscoring that the unformalized algebraic arguments require careful verification.

Claimed proof for CM abelian varieties

Model / AI: OpenAI (unreleased)

•self-reported

An internal OpenAI model produced a proof claiming the rational Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties with complex multiplication (CM). While accompanied by Lean formalizations for parts of the argument, a companion paper on K3 surfaces was retracted due to a sign error, and the CM abelian variety paper remains under specialist review.

Source read 2026-10-08. Read the repository catalogue, retractions notice for related papers, and algebraic geometry family descriptions. The cycles construction was not verified.
