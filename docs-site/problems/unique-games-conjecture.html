Claimed proof and Lean formalization in OpenAI math repository

Model / AI: OpenAI (unreleased)

✓machine-checked•self-reported

OpenAI's release of 722 manuscripts across 372 result families includes a claimed proof of Subhash Khot's 2002 Unique Games Conjecture, supported by Lean proof artifacts. The work was generated by an unreleased internal frontier model using roughly three hours of reasoning compute per family. Prior to release, rumors of the result prompted related preprint releases by human researchers, and the proof remains subject to community review.
