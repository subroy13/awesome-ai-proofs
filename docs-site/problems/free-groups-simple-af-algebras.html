Existence of amenable actions via Baire category

Model / AI: GPT-6 Astra

•self-reported

Yuhei Suzuki resolves the eight-year-old open existence problem for amenable actions of non-amenable groups on classifiable simple C*-algebras by proving that free groups act amenably on unital simple AF-algebras. Suzuki reports substantial assistance from GPT-6 Astra in discovering dynamical constructions and verifying topological density properties.
