Sharp anticoncentration bound via total influence

Alexandr Grebennikov proves the optimal O_d(r^(-1/2)) bound for the polynomial Littlewood–Offord problem, resolving the conjecture of H. Nguyen and V. Vu. The argument also establishes a bound on the total influence of bounded-degree rational functions, settling a conjecture of Kothari et al. The author credits the core argument as having been discovered autonomously by GPT-6 Pro.
