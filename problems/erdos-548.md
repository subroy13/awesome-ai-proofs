## Question

Does every sufficiently edge-dense graph contain every tree of the prescribed size?

## Additional information

The compared Lean theorem uses at least (k-1)n/2 + 1 edges. When (k-1)n is odd this is one edge stronger than the sharp classical hypothesis, so it is a marginally weaker result than the full conjecture.
