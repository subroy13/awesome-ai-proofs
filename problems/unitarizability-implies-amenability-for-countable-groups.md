OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Unitarizability implies amenability for discrete groups'. We prove that a discrete group is amenable if and only if every uniformly bounded representation on a complex Hilbert space is similar to a unitary representation. This resolves Dixmier's unitarizability problem affirmatively for discrete groups.
