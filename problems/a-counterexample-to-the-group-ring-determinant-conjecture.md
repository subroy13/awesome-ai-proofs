OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A Counterexample to the Group-Ring Determinant Conjecture'. We disprove the unrestricted group-ring Determinant Conjecture. We construct a finitely generated group G and a square matrix over \mathbb Z[G] that is invertible over \mathbb Q[G] and has Fuglede–Kadison determinant strictly between zero and one. The logarithmic integral defining the determinant is finite.
