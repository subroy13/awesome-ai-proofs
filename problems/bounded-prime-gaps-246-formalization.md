## Question

Can the Maynard–Polymath proof that infinitely many consecutive primes differ by at most 246 be formalized in Lean?

## Additional information

This is a formalization of an existing theorem, not a new prime-gap bound. The public flagship Lean theorem assumes Bombieri–Vinogradov as a hypothesis because that theorem is not yet in Mathlib.

## Aug 18, 2026 · AxiomProver-assisted formalization

**Model / AI:** AxiomProver

Axiom Math reports that AxiomProver generated machine-checkable Lean proofs from a human-built blueprint, followed by human curation and library work. The repository formalizes the Maynard sieve and the Polymath8b ingredients needed for the bound 246, conditional in Lean on Bombieri–Vinogradov.
