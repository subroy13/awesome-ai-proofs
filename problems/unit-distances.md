## Question

## May 2026 · Unit-distance conjecture disproved

**Model / AI:** OpenAI (unreleased), Sol

an OpenAI reasoning model disproved Erdős's 1946 conjecture by constructing point sets with at least n^(1+δ) unit distances, for a fixed δ > 0 and infinitely many n; outside mathematicians checked it and wrote [companion remarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20695). An early [Lean development](https://github.com/logical-intelligence/erdos-unit-distance) rested on named classical inputs; Boris Alexeev then had OpenAI's Sol autoformalize the result assuming nothing beyond the axioms of mathematics, about 1.2M lines, which [Buzzard compiled himself](https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2026/07/20/human-mathematicians-are-being-outcounterexampled/).
