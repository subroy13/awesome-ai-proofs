## Question

Is every candidate branched covering datum over the Riemann sphere realizable whenever the partition parts satisfy the greatest common divisor and least common multiple conditions?

## Additional information

The paper proves a reduction to the three-branch-point case rather than proving the full conjecture unconditionally. The preprint is unrefereed and unformalized.

## Sep 20, 2026 · Reduction to three branch points via forest partitions

**Model / AI:** GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra

Liu, Sun, Xu, Ye, and Zhou prove that M. E. Zieve's conjecture on realizable branch data reduces to the case of three branch points. The authors report that GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra provided key assistance in constructing inductive partition schemes and verifying edge cases.
