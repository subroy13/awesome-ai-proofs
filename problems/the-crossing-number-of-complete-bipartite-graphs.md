We prove the Zarankiewicz crossing-number conjecture, resolving Turán's brickyard problem. For all positive integers m, n, the ordinary crossing number of the complete bipartite graph K_{m,n} is \displaystyle \left\lfloor\frac m2\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac{m-1}{2}\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac n2\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\right\rfloor.
