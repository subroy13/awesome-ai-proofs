# Large cap sets in eight dimensions

[← Problems](../README.md) · [Definitions and documentation](../docs/visualizations.md)

**Research · Combinatorics · Dec 14, 2023**

**Model / AI:** FunSearch

## Question

How many vectors in F₃⁸ can we choose without three distinct vectors summing to zero?

## Additional information

A larger explicit construction gives a lower bound; it does not determine the maximum cap-set size.

[Problem explanation](../explanations/cap-sets.md)

## Dec 14, 2023 · FunSearch

**Model / AI:** FunSearch

LLM program search found a cap set of size 512 in dimension 8, beating the previous 496. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6).

**Reported evidence (result):** verified.
