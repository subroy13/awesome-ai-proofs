## Question

For every torsion-free group G and field K, does the group ring K[G] contain no non-trivial zero divisors?

## Additional information

The claimed counterexample and characteristic-two direct finiteness arguments are unrefereed. The repository provides partial Lean formalizations, but specialist ring-theoretic audit has not confirmed the non-triviality of the zero divisors or torsion-freeness of the group.
