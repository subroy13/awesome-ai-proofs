## Aug 31, 2026 · Counterexample from a quantum graph algebra

**Model / AI:** ChatGPT 5.6 Sol

Freitag and Mutchnik report that guided conversations with ChatGPT led to a simple theory built from an infinite-dimensional vector space over the fraction division ring of a quantum graph algebra, giving a counterexample to the 1996 conjecture. The authors developed and checked the mathematics.
