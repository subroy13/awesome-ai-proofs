OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'The entropy-rate dimension formula for self-similar measures on the line'. We prove that the Hausdorff dimension of every finite real self-similar measure equals the minimum of one and its random-walk entropy rate divided by its Lyapunov exponent. Exact overlaps are allowed, and the contraction ratios may be unequal and negative. This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture.
