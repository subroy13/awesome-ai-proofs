## Question

Is it NP-hard to distinguish whether a 2-prover 1-round unique game has value at least 1 - epsilon or at most delta, for every epsilon, delta > 0?

## Additional information

This claim is part of OpenAI's unrefereed 722-manuscript release and has not been peer-reviewed or broadly certified by complexity theorists. The public repository includes a Lean artifact, but its soundness and semantic alignment with Khot's original formulation remain under community inspection.
