OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'The weak pinned planar distance theorem'. We prove the weak pinned Erdős distinct-distance conjecture. For every fixed ε > 0, all but o(n) points of any n-point planar set determine at least n^{1-\varepsilon} distinct nonzero distances.
