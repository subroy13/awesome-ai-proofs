## Aug 18, 2026 · Dimension-free constant two

**Model / AI:** GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 5, Danus, Rethlas

Ouyang, Spector and Stockdale report that models developed proof strategies in dialogue with them. The humans redirected a complicated initial route, checked and rewrote the final argument, which proves a dimension-independent weak-type constant at most two and answers Stein's 1986 question.

**Reported evidence (result):** self-reported.
