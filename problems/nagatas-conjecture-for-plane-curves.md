OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Nagata's conjecture for plane curves'. We prove that a nonzero effective plane curve of degree d at r ≥ 10 very general complex points has total multiplicity strictly less than d\sqrt r. The inequality holds simultaneously for all curves, including reducible and nonreduced curves, and establishes Nagata's conjecture in its strict, nonhomogeneous form.
