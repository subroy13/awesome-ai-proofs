OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros'. We prove the uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros. There is an absolute constant c > 0 such that every real zero \beta\in(0,1) of every primitive nonprincipal real Dirichlet L-function of conductor q ≥ 3 satisfies (1-\beta)\log q\ge c.
