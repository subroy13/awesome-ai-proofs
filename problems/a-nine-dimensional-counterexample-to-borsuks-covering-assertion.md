OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A nine-dimensional counterexample to Borsuk's covering assertion'. The compact set of rank-one orthogonal projectors on ℝ4, with the Frobenius metric, cannot be covered by ten sets of strictly smaller diameter. It therefore gives a counterexample to Borsuk's conjecture in dimension nine.
