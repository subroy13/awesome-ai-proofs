## Dec 2021 · A geometric bound on knot signature

**Model / AI:** DeepMind neural networks

Davies and collaborators used supervised learning and feature attribution to identify a connection between knot signature and natural slope. The published result bounds their discrepancy using volume and injectivity radius; the mathematical proof was developed by humans.
