**Model / AI:** OpenAI (unreleased)

OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A counterexample to Ryser's covering conjecture'. For every sufficiently large prime s\equiv2\pmod3 and every sufficiently large odd integer n, with the threshold depending on s, we construct an intersecting (s^n+1)-partite (s^n+1)-uniform hypergraph with covering number s^n+1. This disproves Ryser's covering conjecture in its intersecting case.
