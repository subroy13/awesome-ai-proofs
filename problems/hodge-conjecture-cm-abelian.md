## Question

On a complex abelian variety with complex multiplication, is every Hodge class a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic cycles?

## Additional information

The claimed result covers CM abelian varieties, which is a structured subclass rather than the full general Hodge conjecture. Shortly after release, OpenAI retracted a related companion manuscript regarding K3 surfaces due to a sign error, underscoring that the unformalized algebraic arguments require careful verification.
