# Kourovka 21.29: Common neighbours in generalized Saxl graphs

[← Problems](../README.md) · [Definitions and documentation](../docs/visualizations.md)

**Research · Group theory, Combinatorics, Formal verification · Sep 1, 2026**

**Model / AI:** Codex, ChatGPT Pro, Claude

## Question

Do every two vertices in the relevant generalized Saxl graph of a primitive permutation group have a common neighbour?

## Additional information

The Lean artifact reportedly covers existence of counterexamples at every base size, but not all additional base-two families or diameter computations; no third party audited statement-to-paper fidelity.

## Sep 1, 2026 · Infinite counterexample families

**Model / AI:** Codex, ChatGPT Pro, Claude

Rizzoli and Thomas used several models to find and develop primitive-group counterexamples for every base size, answering Kourovka Problem 21.29 negatively. Codex produced much of the computational code and most of the Lean formalization, while the authors reviewed statements and the completed formalization.

**Reported evidence (every-base-size counterexamples):** machine-checked, self-reported.

**Source review:** 2026-09-08. Read the paper abstract and the linked public source record. The Lean project was not rebuilt and specialist review was not found.
