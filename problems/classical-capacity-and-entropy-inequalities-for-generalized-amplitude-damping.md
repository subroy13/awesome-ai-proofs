OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Classical capacity and entropy inequalities for generalized amplitude damping'. We determine the unassisted classical capacity of every qubit generalized amplitude-damping channel, for all damping strengths and thermal occupations. It equals the one-shot Holevo capacity and is given by a one-variable maximum. A binary pure-state ensemble attains the one-use optimum, and its independent products attain the optimum at every block length. We also prove that one-shot Holevo capacity, minimum output entropy, and regularized unassisted classical capacity are additive under tensor product with every finite-dimensional completely positive trace-preserving partner channel.
