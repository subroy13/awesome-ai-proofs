## Question

Must every sufficiently large finite point set contain four collinear points or six pairwise visible points?

## Additional information

This settles only the (4,6) case, with an enormous explicit threshold. The preprint is unrefereed, unformalized and has no independent check.

## Aug 19, 2026 · Explicit (4,6) threshold

**Model / AI:** GPT-5.6 Sol Pro

Édouard Bonnet reports that after an initial failed attempt and further prompting, GPT-5.6 Sol Pro produced a detailed proof. The paper proves that 10^11055931 points force four collinear points or a six-vertex visibility clique.

**Reported evidence (first open case):** self-reported.
