We construct a counterexample to Kaplansky's direct-finiteness conjecture in odd characteristic. For one specified odd prime p, we obtain a field K of order p4, a finitely generated group G containing torsion, and finite sums a,b\in K[G] with ab=1 but ba\ne1. The same elements define a cellular automaton on KG that is injective but not surjective.
