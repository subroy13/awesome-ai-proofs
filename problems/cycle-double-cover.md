## Jul 2026 · Cycle Double Cover Conjecture

**Model / AI:** GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra

GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra proved the 50-year-old conjecture in a three-page note; the [Lean formalization](https://github.com/openai/cdc-lean) is unconditional, with the Jaeger-Kilpatrick eight-flow theorem formalized in-repo rather than assumed. Geelen and Oum wrote independent expositions ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15399), [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16356)) that reproduce the argument without asserting a correctness check, so the main verification evidence is the Lean proof. Peer review pending.
