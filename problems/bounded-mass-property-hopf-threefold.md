## Question

Do all compact complex manifolds have the bounded mass property?

## Additional information

The unrefereed preprint has no formalization or independent check, and the disclosure credits the AI agent with the initial counterexample rather than every part of the final proof.

## Aug 21, 2026 · Hopf-threefold counterexample

**Model / AI:** Rethlas, GPT-5.6 Sol

Xia and Zhang report that a Rethlas agent using GPT-5.6 Sol constructed the initial counterexample, later improved by a human collaborator. They prove that the bounded mass property fails on the standard Hopf threefold.

**Reported evidence (result):** self-reported.

**Source review:** 2026-09-08. Read the abstract, comments and AI-use disclosure in arXiv v1. The proof was not independently checked.
