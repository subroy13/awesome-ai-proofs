OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Talagrand's discrete-convexity conjecture'. We prove Talagrand's discrete-convexity conjecture. There is a universal integer k such that, whenever an arbitrary family has Bernoulli product measure at least 1-1/k, the sets not contained in a union of k members admit a cover of total cost at most 1/2 at the same density.
