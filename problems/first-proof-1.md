## Feb 2026 · Aletheia on First Proof

**Model / AI:** Aletheia

Google DeepMind's agent solved 6 of the 10 inaugural First Proof problems fully autonomously by majority expert assessment; the graders were academic mathematicians the team recruited, some Google-affiliated, best-of-2 selection used the team's own expertise, and one agent's attempt was rated "Critically Flawed" on a problem the paper counts as solved.
