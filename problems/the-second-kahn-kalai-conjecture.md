OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture'. We prove the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture. For every finite simple graph H with h ≥ 1 edges and at most n vertices, the threshold for G(n,p) to contain an ordinary copy of H is at most C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h), where C is universal. Here p_{\mathrm E}(n,H) is the least density at which every subgraph of H has expected copy count at least one half.
