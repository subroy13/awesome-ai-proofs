OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Maximal Seshadri constants on arbitrary polarized surfaces'. We prove that, for every smooth integral complex projective surface S and every ample line bundle L, the multipoint Seshadri constant at r very general points equals \sqrt{L^2/r} for every sufficiently large integer r. This resolves positively the qualitative Nagata–Biran conjecture for surfaces.
