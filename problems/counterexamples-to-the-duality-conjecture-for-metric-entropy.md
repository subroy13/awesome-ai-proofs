OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Counterexamples to the duality conjecture for metric entropy'. We disprove Pietsch's dimension-free duality conjecture for metric entropy. For every proposed pair of universal constants, we construct origin-symmetric convex bodies that violate the corresponding covering-entropy inequality, already when the covering body is a cube.
