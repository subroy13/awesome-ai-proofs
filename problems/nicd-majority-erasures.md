# Majority optimality in correlation distillation with erasures

[← Problems](../README.md) · [Definitions and documentation](../docs/visualizations.md)

**Research · Probability, Analysis, Theoretical computer science · Oct 2025**

**Model / AI:** GPT-5 Pro

## Question

Among unbiased Boolean functions, does majority maximize the expected absolute multilinear extension under the specified erasure model?

## Additional information

The counterexample concerns five bits at p = 0.40 in the paper’s parameterization. It does not rule out majority optimality in other regimes. Human checking is described by the authors; no formal artifact is listed.

## Oct 2025 · Five-bit counterexample to majority optimality

**Model / AI:** GPT-5 Pro

Ivanisvili and Xie report that GPT-5 Pro found a Boolean function outperforming five-bit majority at p = 0.40. Their paper gives the explicit function and a manual calculation. The authors also prove optimality of majority near p = 0 for each fixed odd dimension.
