## Question

Must entropy production decrease monotonically for space-homogeneous hard-sphere or Maxwell-molecule Boltzmann dynamics?

## Additional information

The counterexamples do not settle the corresponding question for the Landau equation. The preprint is unrefereed, unformalized and lacks an independent check.

## Sep 1, 2026 · Radial Maxwellian-mixture counterexamples

**Model / AI:** ChatGPT 5.6 Sol, Claude

Luis Silvestre reports that ChatGPT produced a first proof using his earlier paper and notes, Claude helped rewrite it, and he reinterpreted and restructured the final argument. Radially symmetric mixtures of Maxwellians show entropy production need not be monotone for hard spheres or Maxwell molecules.
