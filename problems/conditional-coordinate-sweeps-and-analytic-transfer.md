OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Conditional coordinate sweeps and analytic transfer'. For coordinate sweeps with near-uniform line laws and line sizes among powers of two in a suitable fixed interval, we prove a Schatten-moment bound after conditioning on any feasible collection of prescribed card trajectories. An analytic transfer to binary sweeps then shows that a fixed number of sweeps mixes the Thorp shuffle on 2^d cards in O(d) physical steps, matching the order of the support lower bound.
