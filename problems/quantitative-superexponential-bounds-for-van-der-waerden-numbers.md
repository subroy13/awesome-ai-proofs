OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'Quantitative Superexponential Bounds for van der Waerden Numbers'. We prove that there are absolute constants c > 0 and K0 such that W_r(k)\gt k^{ck\lfloor\log_2 r\rfloor} for every k\ge K_0 and r ≥ 2. Consequently W_r(k)^{1/k}\to\infty for each fixed r ≥ 2, giving a quantitative positive resolution of Erdős's superexponential-growth question, including the two-color case.
