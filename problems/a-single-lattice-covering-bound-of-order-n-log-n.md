OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A single-lattice covering bound of order n log n'. Every convex body in ℝn, n ≥ 2, admits a covering by translates along one full-rank lattice with density at most Cn\log n, for an absolute constant C. No symmetry or boundary regularity is assumed.
