OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A translational tile with no fully periodic tiling in dimension three'. We construct a finite translational tile in ℤ3 that admits tilings but no fully periodic tiling. Its unit-cube thickening has the same property in ℝ3, even when arbitrary real translations are allowed. This gives a negative resolution of the periodic tiling conjecture in dimension three.
