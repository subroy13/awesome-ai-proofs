## Question

Is the Rademacher cotype of a bounded linear operator between Banach spaces universally bounded by the maximum of its Gaussian cotype and its (q,1)-summing norm?

## Sep 17, 2026 · Negative resolution for cotype-2 operators

**Model / AI:** ChatGPT (GPT-5.6)

Xinglong Wu resolves Michel Talagrand's operator cotype problem in the negative, demonstrating that the Rademacher cotype cannot be controlled by the Gaussian cotype and (q,1)-summing norm even in the fundamental case q = 2. The author reports that ChatGPT (GPT-5.6) discovered the counterexample structure during interactive experimentation.
