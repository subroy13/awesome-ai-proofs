## Question

Does there exist an absolute constant C such that every graph with no complete minor K_t has chromatic number at most C*t?

## Additional information

The preprint is unrefereed by journal reviewers. The authors provide a Lean 4 formalization of the main theorem in an associated repository, but the formal build has not been independently reproduced for this catalogue.

## Oct 4, 2026 · Linear minor bound with Lean 4 formalization

**Model / AI:** GPT-6 Astra

Sergey Norin and Raphael Steiner prove the Linear Hadwiger Conjecture, showing that K_t-minor-free graphs are O(t)-colorable and settling the linear version of Hugo Hadwiger's 1943 conjecture. The authors state that the proof argument was found by GPT-6 Astra following human conceptual guidance, accompanied by a Lean 4 formal verification.
