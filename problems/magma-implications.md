**Research · Algebra, Automated reasoning · Dec 2025**

**Model / AI:** Classical automated provers

## Question

Which short identities for a set with one binary operation imply which others?

## Additional information

Most reported work used classical automated theorem provers. The finite-magma question is separate from the main implication graph.

## Dec 2025 · Equational Theories Project

**Model / AI:** Classical automated provers

all 22,028,942 non-reflexive implications between the 4694 simplest magma laws determined and formalized in Lean, via explicit proofs and countermodels plus transitive closure, completing the primary goal on 14 April 2025; one case and its dual remain open in the separate finite-magma graph. Most of the work was done by classical automated theorem provers rather than LLMs, and Tao reports that "modern AI tools did not play a major role in this project".
