## Question

Does the Riemann zeta function have a zero-free half-plane of the form Re(s) > theta for an explicit theta strictly less than one?

## Additional information

The claimed result is a zero-free region Re(s) > 7/8, not the full Riemann Hypothesis (which requires Re(s) > 1/2). The manuscript is unrefereed and community auditing of the analytic estimates is in progress.
