OpenAI's internal frontier model proved and formalized in Lean the main result of 'A universal group of type F∞'. We construct a single group of type F∞ containing every finitely presented group. Its finitely generated subgroups, up to isomorphism, are exactly the finitely generated recursively presented groups. This answers the F∞ form of the higher-dimensional Higman embedding question.
